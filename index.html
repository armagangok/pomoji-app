How it works

Aim. Focus. Recharge. Repeat.

The Pomodoro Technique is simple. Sticking to it is the hard part. Pomo makes every step feel like progress.

Step 1

Aim at a target

Tell Pomoji what you are working towards and how many sessions you want today. Your plan is ready in under a minute.

Step 2

Focus for 25 minutes

Start a session and put the phone down. The countdown lives on your Lock Screen and Pomo keeps working next to you.

Step 3

Recharge, then go again

When the alarm rings, take a real break. After four sessions, a longer one. Your streak grows one session at a time.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Pomoji free?

Yes. Pomoji is a free download with the full Pomodoro timer, Lock Screen alarms, the daily goal and the free companions. Pomoji Pro is an optional subscription that adds detailed stats and heatmaps, custom session lengths and sounds, iCloud Sync, and the Pro companions.

What is the Pomodoro Technique?

It is a time-management method where you work in focused sessions, classically 25 minutes, separated by 5-minute breaks, with a longer break after every four sessions. Pomoji runs that rhythm for you and keeps count.

Does the timer keep running when I lock my iPhone?

Yes. Pomoji tracks real time, shows a live countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and alerts you when the session ends even if the app is closed.

Will the alarm ring when my iPhone is on silent?

If you allow alarms, Pomoji uses Apple's AlarmKit so the end of a session rings like a real alarm, full screen on the Lock Screen, even in Silent mode or a Focus. From the alarm you can start your break or your next focus session in one tap.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Your sessions, streaks and settings stay on your iPhone. With Pomoji Pro you can turn on iCloud Sync to keep them in your own iCloud account across your devices.

Does Pomoji track me or show ads?

No. Pomoji has no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.

Which devices does Pomoji support?

Pomoji is made for iPhone and needs iOS 26 or later.

Can I change the session lengths?

Yes. The classic 25/5/15 rhythm is the default, and Pomoji Pro lets you set your own focus, short break and long break lengths.
